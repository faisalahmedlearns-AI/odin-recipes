Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with a baked dough base, tomato sauce, melted cheese, and a variety of toppings. It is crispy on the outside, soft inside, and perfect for a quick and delicious meal.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauc
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Sliced onions
  • Sliced Bell peppers
  • Sliced tomatoes
  • Black olives
  • Oregano
  • Chili flakes

Steps:

  1. Prepare the pizza dough and roll it into a round shape.
    2. \
  2. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  3. Add mozzarella cheese and your preferred toppings.
  4. Sprinkle oregano and chili flakes on top.
  5. Preheat the oven to 200°C
  6. Bake the pizza for 12–15 minutes, or until the cheese melts and the crust becomes golden brown.
  7. Let it cool slightly, slice it, and serve.
