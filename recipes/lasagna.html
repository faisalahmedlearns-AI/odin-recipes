Description

Lasagna is a delicious Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy cheese, and melted mozzarella. It is a comforting and flavorful meal that is perfect for family dinners.


This classic lasagna is easy to prepare and tastes even better when served hot and fresh from the oven.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Minced beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Onion and garlic

Steps

  1. Fry the onion and garlic
  2. Add the minced beef and cook it, then add the tomato sauce.
  3. Start layering: sauce → sheets → cheese
  4. Repeat the layers.
  5. Bake at 180°C for 30-40 minutes.
