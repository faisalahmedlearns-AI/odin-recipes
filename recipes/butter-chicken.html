Butter Chicken

Butter chicken is a popular Indian dish made with tender pieces of chicken cooked in a rich, creamy tomato-based sauce. It is known for its buttery texture, mild spices, and delicious flavor.

Ingredients

  • Boneless chicken
  • Butter
  • Tomato puree
  • Heavy cream
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Garam masala
  • Red chili powder
  • Turmeric powder
  • Salt
  • Fresh coriasnder

Steps:

  1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Cook the chicken until it is lightly browned and fully cooked.
  3. Melt butter in a pan and sauté the onion, garlic, and ginger.
  4. Add the tomato puree and cook until the sauce thickens.
  5. Add garam masala, red chili powder, turmeric, and salt.
  6. Add the cooked chicken and mix well with the sauce.
  7. Pour in the cream and simmer for a few minutes.
  8. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with naan or rice.
